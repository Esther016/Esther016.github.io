Research · Mar 2026 – present · Advised by Prof. Kaizhu Huang

Does the model reason, or answer by habit?

Fine-grained vision-language model evaluation

The question

Fine-R1 is a vision-language model built for fine-grained visual recognition: telling apart categories that look almost the same. When its performance changes, I want to know whether that reflects a reasoning limitation, a systematic response bias, or an artifact of how the evaluation was set up.

What I do

I design controlled experiments on Fine-R1 and its Qwen base model, and build Python pipelines for large-scale closed- and open-world testing. I hold the image and the question fixed, change one evaluation condition at a time, and look at how the answers move and which failure patterns keep recurring.

Where it stands

Ongoing. Results are not public yet.
