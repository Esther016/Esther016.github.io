Research · Sep 2025 – Mar 2026 · Independent study with Prof. Jiahua Yue

Do models answer differently in Chinese and English?

LLM cultural alignment — computational social science

The question

If you ask the same question in English and in Chinese, do large language models give the same answer? And does that depend on where the model comes from, or how the question is framed?

What I built

A model-agnostic bilingual pipeline for large-scale prompting and structured response collection: multi-model API orchestration, retry and checkpointing for long runs, then statistical analysis and visualization.

What gets compared

Model behavior across languages, model origins, and prompting conditions, looking at consistency, framing sensitivity, and cross-lingual differences.

Where it stands

The study is ongoing. Code and results are not public yet.
